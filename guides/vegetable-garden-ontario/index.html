Organic amendments for Ontario vegetable gardens

Short answer: Test your soil first, then mix in organic matter (worm castings or compost), add a calcium source and a phosphorus source at planting, and top up with a nitrogen meal during the season. Follow the dose printed on each bag.

Start with the soil you have

Soil across Ontario ranges from heavy clay to sandy loam, and the right amendments depend on yours. A simple soil test (pH and basic nutrients) tells you whether to raise pH, add calcium or magnesium, or just add organic matter. Without a test, building the soil with organic matter is the safe first step.

A simple plan for a vegetable bed

  1. Build the soil. Mix Compost Blend (1 part compost to 4 parts soil) or Worm Castings into the bed.
  2. Add calcium and fix pH if needed. Oyster Shell Flour (2.5 to 5 lb per 100 sq ft) gives slow calcium. Dolomite Lime raises pH and adds magnesium: about 5 lb per 100 sq ft raises pH from 5.5 to 6.5. Use one or the other based on your soil test, not both blindly.
  3. Feed roots and fruit. Add Bone Meal (1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil) or Soft Rock Phosphate at planting.
  4. Feed leaves. For leafy crops, mix in Feather Meal or Alfalfa Meal and repeat as the bag directs.
  5. Finish with minerals. Kelp Meal and Basalt Rock Dust add potassium and trace minerals.

Prefer one bag? Flower Power 2-6-4 or Level Up 3-3-3 combine several of these. The calculator works out amounts for your bed size.

Heavy clay soil

Gypsum supplies calcium and sulfur and is known for helping loosen clay. Mix 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil in pots or follow the bag for beds. Adding compost every year also improves clay structure.

Containers and raised beds

In pots and raised beds, the doses are given per gallon of soil, so it is easy to scale. Mix the amendment into the soil before planting and water it in.

Questions

When should I add organic amendments to my garden?

Mix them into the soil at planting in spring, and top up during the growing season as each bag directs.

How do I raise soil pH organically?

Dolomite lime raises pH and adds calcium and magnesium. About 5 lb per 100 sq ft raises pH from 5.5 to 6.5, according to the bag.

What helps heavy clay soil?

Gypsum and regular additions of compost or worm castings help improve clay soil.

Do I need a soil test?

It is recommended. A test shows your pH and helps you choose between lime, oyster shell flour and other minerals.
