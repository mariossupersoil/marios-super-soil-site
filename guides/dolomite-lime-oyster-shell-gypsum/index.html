    Dolomite lime, oyster shell and gypsum compared

    Short answer: Dolomite lime raises pH and adds calcium and magnesium. Oyster shell flour gives slow calcium and helps keep pH steady. Gypsum adds calcium and sulfur with little effect on pH.

    At a glance

    AmendmentCalciumAlso providesEffect on pH
    Dolomite Lime21%Magnesium 12%Raises pH
    Oyster Shell Flour34%96% calcium carbonate, micronutrientsHelps keep pH balanced
    Gypsum22%Sulfur 17%Little effect

    Dolomite lime

    Dolomite Lime raises soil pH and adds calcium and magnesium. Use 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil. To raise pH from 5.5 to 6.5, use about 5 lb per 100 square feet.

    Oyster shell flour

    Oyster Shell Flour is 96% calcium carbonate and breaks down gradually, giving lasting calcium. Use 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon for new pots and 1 tablespoon for established plants, or 2.5 to 5 lb per 100 square feet in beds, mixed into the top 3 inches.

    Gypsum

    Gypsum supplies calcium and sulfur with little effect on soil pH, so it suits soil that already has the right pH. It is also used to help loosen clay. Use 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil or coco.

    How to choose

    • Soil is acidic and you want to raise pH: dolomite lime.
    • You want steady calcium without a big pH swing: oyster shell flour.
    • pH is fine but you want calcium and sulfur, or you have heavy clay: gypsum.

    A soil test tells you which applies. See also our vegetable garden plan.

    Questions

    Which amendment raises soil pH?

    Dolomite lime raises pH. Oyster shell flour helps keep pH balanced. Gypsum has little effect on pH.

    What is the difference between lime and gypsum?

    Lime raises pH. Gypsum adds calcium and sulfur without changing pH much.

    How much dolomite lime do I need?

    About 5 lb per 100 sq ft raises pH from 5.5 to 6.5, according to the bag. For pots use 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil.

    Can I use oyster shell flour in pots?

    Yes. Use 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil in new pots and 1 tablespoon for established plants.
