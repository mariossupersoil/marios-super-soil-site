Organic soil amendments in Canada

Short answer: Organic dry amendments are natural, slow-acting ingredients (meals, rock dusts, castings and compost) that you mix into soil to feed plants and soil life over weeks and months. Mario's Super Soil makes them in Mississauga, Ontario, and ships across Canada.

What are organic dry amendments?

Organic dry amendments are natural materials, such as ground bone, crab shell, feathers, seaweed, alfalfa, crushed rock and worm castings, that you work into soil or potting mix. They do not dissolve straight into the soil water like a salt. Soil microbes break them down gradually, so plants are fed steadily over weeks or months.

Because they are dry and slow-acting, they are forgiving. They also feed the living soil, not only the plant.

The four groups of amendments

  • Meals are nutrient sources: Bone Meal, Crab Meal, Feather Meal, Kelp Meal and Alfalfa Meal.
  • Rock dusts and minerals supply calcium, magnesium, sulfur, phosphorus and trace minerals: Soft Rock Phosphate, Gypsum, Oyster Shell Flour, Dolomite Lime and Basalt Rock Dust.
  • Castings and compost add live biology and organic matter: Worm Castings and Compost Blend.
  • Blends combine several ingredients in one bag: Flower Power (2-6-4) and Level Up (3-3-3).

How to choose by what you want to achieve

  • Leafy, green growth (nitrogen): Feather Meal (11-0-0) for a slow, strong source, Alfalfa Meal for a gentler boost, Crab Meal (4-3-0) for nitrogen plus calcium.
  • Roots, flowers and fruit (phosphorus): Bone Meal (4-10-0) or the slower Soft Rock Phosphate.
  • All-round plant strength (potassium): Kelp Meal (1-0-4) and Alfalfa Meal.
  • Calcium, magnesium and trace minerals: Oyster Shell Flour, Dolomite Lime, Gypsum and Basalt Rock Dust.
  • Living, healthy soil: Worm Castings and Compost Blend.

How to use them

Every bag prints the dose, usually in tablespoons per gallon of soil, so there is no guesswork. Mix the amendment into the soil, water it in, and start with the lower end of the range on the bag. Slow-acting meals can take a few weeks to show an effect, so avoid adding more too soon.

For example, Bone Meal is used at 1 to 2 tablespoons per gallon of soil and releases its nutrients over about 4 months.
