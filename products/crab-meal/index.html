Crab meal is an organic fertilizer made from crab & lobster shells, providing nutrients that support healthy soil & flower and vegetable growth.

The NPK ratio of crab meal is 4-3-0, with a high concentration of nitrogen, phosphorus, and magnesium, and it is particularly rich in chitin.

  • Chitin-rich pest shield
  • Fights root rot & blight
  • High calcium (prevents rot)
  • Slow-release nitrogen (4-3-0)
  • Supercharges soil microbes
  • Essential for tomatoes & veg

How to use Comment utiliser

Apply in spring and every 2–3 months. Use 1–2 teaspoons per gallon of soil. Yellow leaves may indicate nitrogen deficiency.

Appliquer au printemps et tous les 2 à 3 mois. Utiliser 1 à 2 cuillères à café par gallon de terre. Les feuilles jaunes peuvent indiquer une carence en azote.

Store in a cool, dry place, away from moisture.
