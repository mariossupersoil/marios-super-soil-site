Kelp is a marine algae sourced from nutrient-rich oceans. Often combined with fish products, it promotes healthier plant growth, improves yields, & enhances garden quality.

With an NPK ratio of 1-0-4, kelp meal releases nutrients quickly and provides over 70 vitamins and minerals, including essential micronutrients and macronutrients.

  • 70+ ocean minerals
  • Heat & drought defense
  • Stimulates strong roots
  • Organic potassium (1-0-4)
  • Feeds soil microbes
  • Ideal for soil, coco & teas
