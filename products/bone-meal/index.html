Bone Meal

Premium quality
4 - 10 - 0Sizes: 2 lb (0.9 kg) · 5 lb (2.3 kg) · 10 lb (4.5 kg)

Bone meal is an organic fertilizer made from ground animal bones, rich in phosphorus to support strong roots & healthy plant growth.

It also provides calcium, iron, magnesium, and zinc, helping promote healthy stems, leaves, flowers, seeds and fruit production.

  • Rich in bio calcium
  • Steady slow release
  • Strong root networks
  • Bigger blooms & fruit
  • Ideal for bulbs, veggies & shrubs
  • High organic phosphorus

How to use Comment utiliser

Apply 1–2 tablespoons per gallon of soil. Mix in and water lightly. Nutrients release over 4 months.

Appliquez 1 à 2 cuillères à soupe par gallon de terreau. Mélangez et arrosez légèrement. Les nutriments se libèrent pendant 4 mois.

Store in a cool, dry place, away from moisture.
