Worm Castings

Premium quality
1.5 - 2.5 - 1.3Net wt. 1.5 lb (1.0 L)

Worm castings are a natural fertilizer produced by earthworms, also known as vermicast. These castings consist of earthworm waste.

As worms consume compost materials, their nutrient-rich waste creates a superior soil amendment that supports healthier plant growth.

  • Packed with live microbes
  • Improves water retention
  • Natural disease resistance
  • 100% safe, never burns
  • Activates dry amendments
  • Ideal for soil, coco & teas

How to use Comment utiliser

Mix into potting soil or sprinkle around plants. For established plants, use 1–2 cups. Water thoroughly.

Mélangez au terreau ou saupoudrez autour des plantes. Pour les plantes établies, utilisez 1 à 2 tasses. Arrosez abondamment.

Store in a cool, dry place, away from moisture.
