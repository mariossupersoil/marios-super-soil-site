    Worm castings and compost are sold in litres and gallons, not by weight, because the weight changes with moisture. Roughly: 1 L is about 1 to 1.3 lb, 1 gallon about 4 to 5 lb, 2 gallons about 8 to 10 lb and 4 gallons about 15 to 20 lb.

    Worm castings are a natural soil amendment produced by earthworms, also known as vermicast. These castings consist of earthworm waste.

    As worms consume compost materials, their nutrient-rich waste creates a superior soil amendment that supports healthier plant growth.

    • Improves water retention
    • Natural disease resistance
    • 100% safe, never burns
    • Activates dry amendments
