    A complete organic gardening kit for new gardeners. It brings together six of our organic dry amendments, so you can feed every stage of growth without choosing one product at a time. The bags are the same ones we sell on their own, with the dose printed on every bag.

    Each kit has six bags: 6 x 2 lb in the 2 lb kit and 6 x 5 lb in the 5 lb kit. Ships across Canada.

    What is in the kit

    • Level UpAll-round balanced growth. 1 tbsp per gallon. Mature plants: 2 tbsp. Seedlings: 1/2 tbsp.
    • Flower PowerFor blooms and bigger yields. 1 tbsp per gallon. Mature plants: 2 tbsp. Seedlings: 1/2 tbsp.
    • Kelp MealOcean minerals for stress and root growth. 1–2 tbsp per gallon of soil, monthly in the growing season.
    • Alfalfa MealGentle nitrogen and a growth boost. 2 tbsp per gallon of soil, in spring and every 6 weeks.
    • Compost BlendOrganic matter that builds humus. 1 part compost to 4 parts soil, or spread on beds and lawns.
    • Worm CastingsLive biology for any soil. Mix into potting soil, or 1–2 cups for established plants.
