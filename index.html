    Our story

    Mario's Super Soil started in 2019 as a basement operation in Mississauga, Ontario. We began by experimenting with synthetic nutrients for growing cannabis, then moved to organic gardening because it fit our values better.

    We found that high costs and limited availability kept organic dry amendments out of reach for many growers, so we set out to make them accessible, affordable and effective. We also want to encourage younger people to take up gardening as a life skill.

    We believe nature provides the best path to plant health, helping gardeners of all levels cultivate a vibrant, sustainable future, one garden at a time.

    Questions

    What makes your products different?

    Low usage rates: Level Up 3-3-3 and Flower Power 2-6-4 use 1 tablespoon per gallon of soil, so a bag goes a long way. The bags are recyclable and reusable, and everything is made locally in Mississauga, Ontario.

    Are your products organic?

    Yes. All products are made from organic ingredients to improve soil health and plant growth, with no synthetic chemicals.

    Can beginners use them?

    Yes. Every bag has clear usage instructions, and the Super Starter Pack is built for new gardeners.

    Do they work indoors and outdoors?

    Yes. They work for indoor and outdoor gardens, year-round.

    Can I use them in hydroponic systems?

    They work in soil-based systems and coco coir setups that include nutrient amendments. They are not suitable for traditional liquid-only hydroponic systems.

    Do they change soil pH?

    Some do. Dolomite lime helps balance pH, while gypsum makes nutrients more available without significantly changing it.

    How much of the other amendments should I use?

    Gypsum: 1–2 tablespoons per gallon of soil or coco. Dolomite lime: 5 lb per 100 sq ft to raise pH by one point. Oyster shell flour: 2.5–5 lb per 100 sq ft, mixed into the top 3 inches of soil.

    Can I plant in the empty bag?

    Yes. The bags are reusable, so you can fill them with soil and plant directly in them.

    How long does shipping take?
